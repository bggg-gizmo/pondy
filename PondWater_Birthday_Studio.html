Privacy Policy

Birthday Studio is an offline greeting-card editor. Card contents and imported pictures are processed only on this device. The application does not transmit them to any server or maintain an account, message history, or persistent editing records.

There is no advertising, analytics, geolocation, microphone or camera access, or contact collection. Pictures are exported only when you choose to save them using the system document picker. The exported files can be accessed by anyone you share them with.
