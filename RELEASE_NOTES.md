# Pondy 1.1.0 🎂
**PondWater — a birthday & greeting card maker**  
**By the Background Gremlin Group**

Meet Pondy: a colorful, offline-friendly way to make thoughtful birthday and greeting cards right from your Android phone.

## What's inside

🎨 **Five beautiful themes** — Sweet Rose, Blue Skies, Golden Day, Garden Party and Starry Night.  
💌 **Your message, your style** — personalize recipients, greetings, occasions, signatures and lettering.  
📸 **Bring your photos** — use a local photo as your card background.  
🖼️ **Keep and share** — export finished cards to PNG.  
📱 **Made for Android** — portrait-first layout and a refined, black-backed adaptive launcher icon.  
📴 **Works offline** — no account, no ads, no network permission.

## Compatibility

Android 8.0 and above (API 26+). Signed Android APK. No runtime Android permissions required.

## Install

Download the version 1.1.0 APK from the release assets, open it on your Android device and follow the normal installation prompt.

## Source and technical notes

The standalone HTML editor and source build information are in the [repository](https://github.com/bggg-gizmo/pondy).

Builds use the Java compiler, D8, AAPT2, zipalign and apksigner directly. **No Gradle. No GitHub Actions.**

## Verification

The packaged DEX, manifest, alignment and APK signatures were checked, and the card editor has browser regression coverage. Physical-device verification remains outstanding.
