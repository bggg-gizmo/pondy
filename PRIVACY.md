# Privacy Information

**Pondy — PondWater Birthday & Greeting Card Maker**  
**Background Gremlin Group** · Version 1.1.0

Pondy is designed to work offline. Its Android package does not request internet, location, contact, camera or external storage permissions.

## Your cards

Recipient names, greetings, signatures, chosen themes and selected photographs are processed on the device to render a greeting card. Pondy does not upload card contents to a remote server or require an account.

## Local processing

Essential app settings may be stored locally. Pondy does not include an ad network, telemetry service or automatic card-history synchronization.

## Photos and exported images

The Android system document picker handles imports and exports. You choose which photograph to use and where to save the exported PNG. Original photo metadata is not directly copied into the exported card image; exported metadata is recreated by the editor.

Once you share a saved card using another app, that app's data processing rules apply. Pondy cannot control third-party copies.

## Contact

Questions and bug reports: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/bggg-gizmo/pondy/issues). Please do not attach personal card photos or private information to public reports.
